## Remaining limits

This work does not establish a guaranteed rubric score, human validity, an error-free benchmark, or effectiveness on real investigative records. The label review and exclusions are exploratory, and shared AI biases remain possible. Additional AI labels alone would not remove these limits. The most valuable next evidence would be representative human adjudication and a format-controlled generation study.
